The instrumentation recommendations in this report were implemented after review on July 17, 2026:

## Executive Conclusion

Do not change either scoring formula yet.

The current hit model continues to rank the top of the slate effectively. Its audit top 10 produced nearly the same result in the prior and new samples: 75.4% and 75.0%, respectively.

The current HR model shows substantially better separation in the new sample. Its audit top 20 produced a 26.2% HR rate against a 12.2% rate below rank 20.

The strongest next change is audit instrumentation, not formula tuning. The saved workbook does not preserve enough current-versus-classic metadata to evaluate the comparison experiment directly.

## Exploratory Ablations

Alternative rankings were reconstructed from the exported, three-decimal single-trip rates. These tests are approximate because of rounding and because some formula inputs are not exported.

### Hit Model

- Removing game environment raised reconstructed top-20 results from 69.2% to 71.5% in the prior workbook and from 74.4% to 75.6% in the new workbook.
- The same removal lowered the new top-5 result from 77.5% to 75.0%.
- Removing or damping contact, OPS, or TTO did not produce a consistent improvement across both samples and cutoffs.

Interpretation: game environment is the only plausible hit-factor simplification, but its current range is only 0.99-1.02 and the ablation is not consistent enough to justify changing it now.

### HR Model

- Removing or damping park sometimes improved top-5 or top-10 results, but not consistently across top-20 results and both samples.
- Removing or damping pitcher risk similarly moved a few top slots without a stable improvement.
- Removing power or TTO was not supported by the factor evidence.

Interpretation: keep the HR formula unchanged and continue monitoring park and pitcher risk.

## Current Versus Classic Experiment Limitation

The visible list is a union of the current and classic top lists, sorted by the best rank from either model. However, the saved payloads in `index.html` and `hr.html` preserve only the merged display rank and current probability. They discard:

- `currentRank`
- `classicRank`
- `classicProb`
- `inCurrentTop`
- `inClassicTop`

Therefore, this workbook cannot directly answer whether current-only, classic-only, or overlapping selections performed best.

The merged display rank should not be interpreted as the current model rank. For example, new saved hit picks produced:

- Merged display ranks 1-5: 60.0%
- Merged display ranks 6-10: 80.6%
- Merged display ranks 11-20: 68.2%

That apparent inversion is not present in the current full-slate audit, where ranks 1-5 produced 77.5%. It is primarily a measurement and presentation issue, not evidence that the current formula ranks backward.

Using same-day full-slate audit rank as an imperfect proxy:

- Hit saved picks inside the audit top 10: 71.1% (38 picks)
- Hit saved picks outside the audit top 10: 70.2% (57 picks)
- HR saved picks inside the audit top 10: 28.2% (39 picks)
- HR saved picks outside the audit top 10: 22.2% (27 picks)

This proxy is not an exact current-versus-classic test because the historical full-slate audit skips recent-form refinements and historical handedness splits.

## Audit Script Limitation

`audit/audit_stats.py` currently builds its headline rank buckets before splitting rows by `Source`. Consequently, saved picks and full-slate audit rows are pooled, and players appearing in both can be counted twice. With the merged current/classic list, this can make the rank report misleading.

Rank performance should be reported separately for:

1. Full-slate audit rows
2. Saved picks
3. Current-only saved picks
4. Classic-only saved picks
5. Picks selected by both models

## Recommended Next Changes

Do not change scoring constants from this workbook.

Instead, update both pages and the audit export to retain:

- Current rank and probability
- Classic rank and probability
- Selected-by-current flag
- Selected-by-classic flag
- Merged display position as a separate field

Then update `audit/audit_stats.py` to separate sources, deduplicate same-date/player rows where appropriate, and report current-only versus classic-only outcomes.

For betting evaluation, also record the offered American odds and whether a bet was actually placed. Prediction accuracy alone cannot establish profitability; the relevant comparison is model probability versus vig-adjusted market probability and realized return.

Collect at least 10-15 additional slates with the expanded metadata before tuning formulas again.

## Statistical Cautions

- The newest sample contains only eight slates.
- Outcomes are clustered by game and date, and repeated players appear across dates.
- Saved-pick confidence intervals are wide: hit picks 69.4% with an approximate 95% interval of 59.7-77.6%; HR picks 25.8% with an approximate interval of 16.7-37.4%.
- Simple row-level significance tests overstate certainty because rows are not fully independent.
- Factor comparisons are observational and correlated; they are useful for screening but not definitive weight estimates.
- The audit backtest is an approximation of the live model because it skips some historical refinements.
